# Risks and limitations

Likelihood (L) and impact (I) are rated H/M/L. "Known limitation" means it can't be fully solved; the plan is to make it visible and keep its effect small.

| # | Risk or limitation | L | I | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Trace logs are deleted after about 24 h**, and history only builds up while a browser tab is open. | H | H | Catch-up on every connect. Gaps are recorded and shaded in every chart, so a gap never looks like a drop in activity. "Open once a day" guidance. P3: a desktop tray collector or an optional team collector for continuous history. |
| 2 | **Trace text is visible only to System Administrators** (a recent platform change). | H | M | The capability probe detects it and explains it. The explorer, dashboard, exceptions and timeline all work without trace text. Positioned as a dev/test tool where developers are usually admins. The docs are open about the helper's limitation. |
| 3 | **`plugintracelog` has no record ID**, so sync-only saves can't be linked to a record exactly. | H | H | Exact anchors whenever a system job exists (R5), the helper header (R8), and watch mode (time and record known). Otherwise inferred from audit timing (I1), with a confidence shown. Never drawn as exact. |
| 4 | **Timestamps may only have whole-second precision** (spike S1), which blurs the waterfall. | M | H | Spike first. Ordering relies on depth, request, stage and rank, which are exact; bars use the exact durations; a precision indicator is shown; ties go to "ambiguous" groups instead of guessed nesting. |
| 5 | **Flow run data is incomplete or delayed, user-owned, and has no trigger record.** | H | M | Flow links are always inferred, with confidence and evidence. `flowevent` gaps are shown. There's a caveat when the user can't see other people's flows. "Didn't fire" is never claimed on flow data alone; the status is "unknown" instead. |
| 6 | **Undocumented internals** (the `callbackregistration`↔flow mapping, the `clientdata` trigger schema) change. | M | M | Parsers are isolated and tolerant, backed by fixture tests. If parsing fails, the feature degrades to "unknown" instead of a wrong answer. Inferred features sit behind flags. |
| 7 | **Throttling** during catch-up on large environments. | M | M | A request budget, at most 4 concurrent requests, honouring `Retry-After`, a metadata-first lane, and resumable watermarks. A progress bar shows the estimated time left. |
| 8 | **Importing a solution** isn't allowed in some environments (strict production governance). | M | M | Aimed at dev and test, where importing is normal. It's a managed solution with no plugins, tables or data, so it uninstalls cleanly and is easy to review. Demo mode needs nothing. The XrmToolBox tool (P3) covers environments where imports are blocked. |
| 8b | **Running as web resources** hits a platform limit (worker loading, CSP, the 5 MB file limit, versioned paths). | M | H | Spike S6 in M0, before anything else is built. Code splitting and a CI size check. Fallback: the worker delegates `fetch` to the main thread. |
| 9 | **Switching tracing to All** on a busy or production environment adds load and storage, and the restore might not happen. | L | H | Offered only to System Administrators, always with explicit consent. Environment name and type are shown. Restore happens on stop, on tab close, and on the next start (pending-restore marker). Never automatic. |
| 10 | **Sensitive data** in local storage and in exported files (trace text can contain PII or secrets). | M | H | Everything is local, and there's no telemetry. Retention settings and "Forget environment". Redaction with a preview on export. Imports are shown as text only. The docs have a dedicated "data handling" page. P3: an encrypted store. |
| 11 | **IndexedDB eviction or quota** loses history. | M | M | `navigator.storage.persist()`, a storage meter, compressed blobs, rollups that outlive raw rows, and export or backup of the whole environment store. |
| 12 | **Wrong inferences** undermine trust ("it said this flow was caused by my save"). | M | H | Conservative defaults (Low links hidden), one-to-one assignment, the ambiguity penalty, evidence shown on every inferred link, calibration published, and a "report a wrong link" issue template that captures a fixture. |
| 13 | **Scope creep** for a solo developer (many sources and many views). | H | H | Strict milestones. The v0.1 MVP is the explorer, the correlation timeline (exact links), the basic dashboard and demo mode. Each milestone ships and is demoable on its own. |
| 14 | **Performance** with large histories (100k+ rows, large blobs). | M | M | Worker processing, a columnar index, virtualisation, lazily loaded compressed blobs, rollups, performance budgets in CI. |
| 15 | **Microsoft ships a comparable tool.** | L | M | Plugin monitoring has been deprioritized. The cross-source, inference-aware approach is hard to copy quickly. Even so, the project's portfolio value doesn't depend on market share. |
| 16 | **Sovereign clouds and on-premises** aren't supported. | — | L | Stated in the docs. The authority and endpoints are configurable in the architecture for later. |
| 17 | **Bypass headers** (`BypassCustomPluginExecution`, `BypassBusinessLogicExecution`) mean a step "should" have fired but didn't, and nothing in the data says why. | M | L | Expected vs. actual lists "a bypass header on the request" as a possible reason when a step with `canbebypassed` is missing and nothing else explains it. |
| 18 | **Clock skew** between Dataverse, the async service and the flow service distorts cross-service time windows. | M | L | Tolerances in the inferred rules (−2 s before commit is allowed). The timeline marks cross-service offsets as approximate. |
